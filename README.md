# Gobble Dash — The Great Turkey Escape

Thanksgiving is coming — and you are the dinner. Run the turkey 3000 metres
from the farmyard, through the barn and the kitchen, to the forest. Jump the
fences, duck the swinging carving knives, dodge the hot ovens, grab corn,
hide in haystacks and keep ahead of the farmer chasing behind you.

Play it with `node serve.ts` and open http://localhost:8080 — Space or click
to jump, Down to duck, P to pause, M to mute.
